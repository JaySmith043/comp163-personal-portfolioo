# College Life Adventure Game

## Game Concept
This is a text-based game where you play as a college student. You make choices about your course load, what subject to study, and whether to attend a study group. Your GPA, stress level, study hours, and social points change based on your decisions. At the end, the game gives you one of several possible endings.

---

##  Branching Logic Used
- **Comparison operators**: Used in course load decision (check GPA before applying changes).
- **Membership operators (in / not in)**: Used to validate subject choices.
- **Logical operators (and, or, not)**: Used for complex subject study conditions.
- **Identity operators (is, is not)**: Used for type checking in the final decision.
- **Nested if statements**: Used to determine multiple endings.
